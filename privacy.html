Privacy Policy

Rahul Library app (राहुल सार्वजनिक वाचनालय) · Developed by PapaCode · Effective 5 November 2026

In short: we collect only what the library needs to run memberships and lend books. We never sell your information, show ads, or track you. Your ID photo is seen only by the library's admin. You can delete your account in the app at any time once your books are returned (how to delete your account).

Who we are

The Rahul Library app is used by राहुल सार्वजनिक वाचनालय (Rahul Sarvajanik Vachanalay), a public library in Solapur district, Maharashtra, India, to manage its members and books. The app is developed and run for the library by PapaCode (Udyam registration UDYAM-MH-32-0308912), Solapur, Maharashtra, India. In this policy, "we" means the library and PapaCode.

What we collect

WhenWhat
When you apply for membership Full name, date of birth, address, mobile number, email address, username and password (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash), a photo of yourself, a photo of an identity document of your choice (for Aadhaar, the masked version with only the last 4 digits visible) and its type, your drawn signature, and the date you gave consent.
While you are a member Books issued to you and returned, due dates, your book requests and suggestions, and any changes you make to your profile. If you use them: books you save to your wishlist, how far you have read a book (your reading progress and note), comments and photos you post on books, and whether your account is public or private.
On your phone A sign-in token and your profile are kept in the app so you stay signed in. They are removed when you sign out. The app does not back them up to Google.
For notifications If you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone, linked to your account, so we can tell you when a book is issued to you (and tell staff about new requests, suggestions and applications). Notifications can show names and book titles, including on your lock screen. Signing out removes the token.
On our servers Our hosting provider keeps short technical logs (such as the time of a request and the address it came from) to keep the service running and secure.

The app does not access your location, contacts or photo gallery. It only uses a picture when you take or choose one yourself. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

How we use it

  • To review your membership application and confirm your identity before approving it.
  • To lend you books, keep track of what is with you, and show you your own books and requests.
  • To contact you about your books (for example, due dates) and to send password-reset codes to your email.
  • To keep the library's registers and reports.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own details, photo and books in the app.
  • Library staff (librarians and admins) can see members' details, photo, signature and books, to run the library.
  • Only the admin can see your ID photo.
  • Other members can see, in the app's Reading Galaxy, members' names and how many books they read this year. If you make your account public (Profile › Details › Privacy), they can also see the titles of the books you borrowed and when. Accounts are private unless you choose otherwise.
  • Your wishlist is seen only by you. Your reading progress is seen only by you and library staff.
  • Comments and photos you post on a book are seen by you and library staff first; once an admin approves one, every member can see it with your name. You can delete your own comments at any time.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising. We use these service providers only to run the app: Render (app server and database hosting), Supabase (private storage for photos and signatures), and Google Gmail and Brevo (to deliver emails such as password-reset codes), and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver notifications). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Applications that are not approved within 3 days are deleted automatically, together with the photo, ID photo and signature. A rejected application is deleted straight away.
  • Members' information is kept while you are a member.
  • When you delete your account, your login, address, photo, ID photo and signature are erased at once. Your name, mobile number, email address and date of birth are kept with your past loan records, as part of the library's lending register. You can ask us to remove those too (see below).

Children

Members under 18 may apply only with a parent's or guardian's agreement, given in the application form.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, photos and signatures are kept in private storage that only our server can read, and only staff can see member details (only the admin can see ID photos). Sign-in is limited after repeated wrong passwords.

Your choices

  • See and correct your details on the app's Profile page.
  • Turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings for the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile › Delete my account), or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by writing to us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
