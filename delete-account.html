Delete your account

Rahul Library app (राहुल सार्वजनिक वाचनालय) · Developed by PapaCode

Please return all your library books first. An account with books still issued can't be deleted until they are back.

In the app

  1. Open the Rahul Library app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email rahul.vachanalaya@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Rahul Library account", and include your username or mobile number. We confirm the request is yours and delete the account within 30 days.

Staff accounts (librarians and admins) are removed by the library's admin.

What is deleted

  • Your login (username and password). You can never sign in to this account again.
  • Your address.
  • Your photo, the photo of your ID, and your signature.

What is kept, and why

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and date of birth stay with your past loan records, as part of the library's lending register. You can ask us by email to remove these too. We will, unless the library has to keep them for its records.

Applications

A membership application that the library does not approve within 3 days is deleted automatically, with its photo, ID photo and signature. To withdraw an application sooner, email us.
